# Benchmark Tasks by Dataset

## NIST Genomics PPFL
Use at least one predictive or classification task to ensure the privacy benchmark still carries nontrivial utility.

## PRISM
Prefer drug-response prediction, ranking, or retrieval tasks that reflect downstream scientific usefulness.

## TCGA
Use subtype classification and at least one omics interpretation-oriented task.

## MIMIC-IV demo
Use simple healthcare-style sanity-check tasks to validate pipeline behavior and privacy-unit handling.
