# NIST Genomics PPFL Competitor Pack

## Role in validation
Primary privacy stress-testing benchmark.

## Why included
This is the first benchmark DIGIT should face if the claim is safe scientific use of sensitive biological data.

## Validation focus
- privacy attacks under realistic genomic sensitivity
- repeated-query behavior
- comparison against DP baselines
- red-team style analysis where possible

## Minimum tasks
- at least one classification-style utility task
- at least one privacy attack suite using matched splits

## Notes
Keep this benchmark privacy-first. Utility matters here, but the main goal is to test whether DIGIT breaks under a hard biological privacy setting.
