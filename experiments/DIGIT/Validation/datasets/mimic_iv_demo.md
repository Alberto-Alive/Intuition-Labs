# MIMIC-IV Demo

## Role in validation
Healthcare-style sanity-check benchmark before applying for full-access clinical datasets.

## Why included
This provides a realistic medical-data evaluation shape without depending immediately on full credentialed access.

## Validation focus
- patient-level privacy-unit thinking
- pipeline testing on healthcare-like structure
- early healthcare utility sanity checks

## Candidate tasks
- simple risk or outcome prediction proxies
- cohort-style retrieval or classification tasks

## Notes
Treat this as a rehearsal benchmark. It is useful for process validation and early privacy testing, not as the final word on clinical deployment.
