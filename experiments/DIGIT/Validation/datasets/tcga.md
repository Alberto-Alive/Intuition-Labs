# TCGA / GDC Benchmark

## Role in validation
Primary omics realism benchmark.

## Why included
TCGA helps test DIGIT on biologically meaningful high-dimensional data with realistic disease structure.

## Validation focus
- subtype classification
- biomarker-style association tasks
- privacy / utility tradeoff on omics-scale data

## Candidate tasks
- cancer subtype classification
- survival-related risk stratification proxy tasks
- feature-significance preservation under DIGIT constraints

## Notes
Keep tasks reproducible and clearly bounded. Avoid making clinical claims from benchmark experiments.
