# PRISM Repurposing Dataset

## Role in validation
Primary drug-discovery utility benchmark.

## Why included
DIGIT is being positioned for scientific environments relevant to drug development. PRISM gives a direct utility test on drug-response style prediction or ranking tasks.

## Validation focus
- performance retention vs raw baseline
- performance retention vs DP baselines
- sensitivity of results to bottleneck strictness

## Candidate tasks
- drug sensitivity prediction
- response ranking
- retrieval of similar response profiles

## Notes
Use PRISM to show whether privacy protection preserves practically useful scientific signal.
