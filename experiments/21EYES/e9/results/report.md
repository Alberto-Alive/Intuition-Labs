## What Codex Tried

Implemented causal four-stream junction variants with learned bottleneck tokens that compress prefix avenue states, then redistribute through per-avenue cross-reads. Mandatory variants covered one middle junction, every-layer junctions, every-two-layer junctions, and a forced final-junction output path.
The `scout_best_custom` variant was chosen to attack the two prior failure modes directly: avenue collapse and optional junction use. It combines every-layer junctions, gated cross-reads, forced final-junction output, light avenue dropout, leave-one-avenue training, and a small diversity loss.

## Conclusion

not promising: all junction variants underperformed the baseline transformer, and junction controls did not reduce accuracy

## Failure Analysis

Junction gains should be discounted if parameter matching wins, junction zero/shuffle/random controls do not reduce accuracy, max single-avenue accuracy matches the full model, leave-one drops are zero, avenue usage collapses, pairwise avenue cosine is high, or junction utilization/norm indicates an unused bottleneck.

## Next Recommended Experiment

Scale only variants that beat both baselines and show positive full-minus-single-avenue gaps plus nonzero junction-control drops. For the next pass, rerun seeds 0-2 and sweep final-junction forcing strength, `junction_tokens` in {4, 8, 16}, and `lambda_div` in {0.001, 0.01}.
